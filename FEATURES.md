## Features

### Syringes
- **Trigger to inject** - Bring the syringe to your chest and pull the trigger to inject.
- **Smaller syringes on the sleeve** - Stored syringes look smaller and grow back when you grab them.

### Weapons
- **Manual holsters** - Let go of a weapon and it drops; let go at a free holster to put it away (turn "Automatic holsters" off).
- **Flick to drop** - With automatic holsters, flick your hand as you let go to drop the weapon instead.
- **Nearest holster** - With automatic holsters, a weapon flies to the free holster nearest to your hand.
- **All your grenades on the chest** - Every grenade you carry sits in a row on your chest; take the one you want.
- **Grab distances** - Choose how close your hand must be to take your gear, so you don't grab it by accident.
- **Realistic grenade throws** - Grenades fly with your hand's speed and spin and bounce according to their weight.
- **Pulling the pin with your teeth** - Choose how close to your mouth a grenade must get to pull its pin.
- **Precise two-handed grip** - Your free hand only grabs the front grip when it is really close.
- **Manual or automatic cocking** - In manual mode you cock the weapon and work the bolt yourself.
- **Second grip first** - With your hand at your weapon's front grip, nearby weapons and ammo are ignored.
- **Helmet-safe back holsters** - Touching your helmet no longer pulls a weapon from your back.

### Movement
- **Crouch with the turn stick** - Push the turn stick down to crouch and up to stand.
- **Slower when crouched** - You move slower while crouched.
- **Arm-swing sprint** - Swing your arms like a runner to sprint.
- **Always sprint** - Run just by pushing the stick; you walk while aiming, throwing or shooting.
- **No sprinting while aiming or shooting** - Aiming, raising a grenade or shooting slows you to a walk.
- **Progressive sprint** - Sprinting builds up to full speed instead of starting at top speed.
- **Faster sprint** - Raise the top sprint speed up to x1.5.
- **Realistic turns** - Sharp turns while sprinting cost speed; running sideways or backwards is slower.
- **Steady speed on stairs** - Stairs and slopes no longer speed you up.
- **Slide** - Crouch while sprinting to slide.
- **Jump (experimental)** - Push the turn stick up to jump onto tables, rocks and crates.
- **Hard landings** - After a big fall you bend down on landing and feel it in the controllers.
- **Fairer fall damage** - Short falls don't hurt, and damage grows with the height.

### Haptics
- **Damage vibration** - The controllers vibrate when you're hit, on the side of the impact.
- **Weapon handling vibration** - Feel magazines, clips, shells and bullets go in.
- **Grab zone vibration** - A light tap tells you when your hand can take a holstered weapon or your gear.
- **Out of ammo vibration** - Three short taps when you reach for ammo and have none left.

### Cutscenes
- **Skip cutscenes** - Hold both triggers and both grips for a second to skip.
- **Fast-forward dialogue** - The same gesture speeds through dialogue scenes.

## Fixes

### Grabbing
- **Grab guide not matching the blue object** - You now grab what your hand's guide points at.

### Weapons
- **Front grips grabbed from far away** - Your free hand could grab a front grip from two metres away.
- **Automatic cocking lost with the precise grip** - Taking the front grip cocks the weapon again as it should.
- **Loose front grips** - A front grip that falls off is put back.
- **Weapons pulled instead of your helmet** - Grabbing near your head no longer takes a weapon from your back.

### Syringes
- **Accidental grabs from your body** - Your gear is no longer grabbed by mistake when your hands pass near it.
- **Accidental injections** - Syringes no longer inject just by touching your chest.

### Respawning
- **Wrong direction after respawning** - You always face the checkpoint's direction.
- **View turning after respawning** - Your view no longer turns by itself after respawning.

### Cutscenes
- **Unskippable scenes** - Many scenes the game did not let you skip can now be skipped or sped up.
