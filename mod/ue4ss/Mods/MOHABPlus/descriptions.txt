# MOHAB Plus option descriptions.
# Shown by the launcher (info icon next to each option) and by the in-game menu (X on an option).
# Format: "[option_key]" and then one paragraph per line. Keep it plain ASCII (the in-game font has no accents) and
# short: the in-game menu shows about 13 lines of 46 characters. The default value is added automatically.

# --- Syringes ---
[syringe_require_trigger]
A syringe only injects when you bring it to your chest AND pull the trigger of the hand holding it.
Original game: it injects as soon as it touches your chest, which also happens by accident while you move it around.

[syringe_trigger_threshold]
How far the trigger must be pulled to inject a syringe (0.1 = barely touched, 1.0 = pulled all the way).
Only used with "Trigger to inject" on.

[syringe_small_holstered]
Syringes stored on your sleeve are drawn smaller, so they don't cover your arm. They get their normal size back as soon as you grab one.

[syringe_holster_scale]
Size of the syringes on your sleeve (1.0 = normal size).
Only used with "Smaller on the sleeve" on.

# --- Grabbing / weapons ---
[grab_aim]
The object lit in blue, and the one you take, is the one your hand's guide points at, also from a distance. The pin of the grenade in your other hand is taken as soon as your hand is at it. With your hand close to a weapon in a holster (hip or shoulder, within the holster grab distance), you take that weapon, not one on the floor your hand points at.
Original game: it lit the object nearest to a point in front of your hand, often not the one you pointed at.

[manual_holster]
Letting go of a weapon drops it to the floor. To put it away, take it to a free holster where it fits and let go: pistols at the hip, long weapons at either shoulder, grenades on the chest.
Original game: a weapon you let go of flies back to its holster by itself.

[holster_store_distance]
How close to a holster your hand must be for a weapon you let go of to be put away instead of dropped. A light tap tells you when you are close enough: it comes when your hand stops at the holster (not when it passes by), once while it rests there.
Grenades don't use it: they go in at the row of grenades on your chest, 5 cm farther than the inventory grab distance.
Only used with "Manual holsters" on.

[grenade_row]
Every grenade you carry (up to 3) is shown in a row on your chest. Take the one you want: the closest one grows and you feel a light tap.
Original game: only one grenade is shown at a time.

[grenade_row_scale]
Size of the grenades on your chest (1.0 = normal size). They get their normal size back when you grab one.
Only used with "All grenades on chest" on.

[body_grab_distance]
How close your hand must be to take grenades, mission objects and syringes you carry. Smaller: fewer grabs by accident when your hands pass near your body.
Tip: raise it if you often miss what you reach for.

[holster_grab_distance]
How close your hand must be to take a weapon from a holster (hip pistol and the like). Weapons on your back and shoulders keep the game's distance.
Tip: lower it if you pull weapons out by accident.

[weapon_grip_reach]
How close your free hand must be to a weapon's front grip to hold it with both hands.
Original game: up to about two metres, so the second hand could grab the grip from far away.

[grenade_physics]
A grenade flies at the speed and spin of your hand and bounces by its weight: the metal grenade rolls, the stick grenade a bit less, the Gammon bag hardly at all.
Original game: thrown twice as fast as your hand, and grenades stop dead on the floor.

[grenade_throw_strength]
Throw speed, times the speed of your hand (1.0 = exactly as fast as your hand).
Tip: 1.2 feels better in play; raise it if your throws fall short.
Only used with "Realistic grenade throws" on.

[teeth_pin_distance]
How close a grenade in your hand must get to your mouth to pull its pin with your teeth (0 = never).
Original game: 10 cm, so the pin often comes out by accident when you take a grenade from your chest.

[cocking]
Manual: after a reload you pull the bolt or slide yourself, and letting go of a bolt halfway no longer finishes the cycle for you.
Automatic: the game does it when you grab the front grip (original game).

[holster_below_eyes]
With your hand above your eyes (touching your helmet or hat) no weapon is pulled from a holster.
Original game: grabbing near your head took a weapon from your back instead of your helmet.

# --- Haptics ---
[haptics_damage]
Both controllers vibrate when you get hurt, stronger for big hits.

[haptics_intensity]
Strength of all the vibrations added by the mod (damage, reloading, grab zones, out of ammo, hard landings).
Tip: lower it if the vibrations feel too strong.

[haptics_duration]
How long the damage vibration lasts. Big hits last a bit longer.
Only used with "Vibrate on damage" on.

[haptics_directional]
The controller on the side the hit came from vibrates harder, so you can tell where you are being shot from.
Only used with "Vibrate on damage" on.

[haptics_weapons]
Feel magazines, clips, shells and bullets going in, and break-action shotguns opening and closing.

[haptics_no_ammo]
Three short taps on the hand that presses grip at your magazine pouch when you have no ammo left for your weapon.

[haptics_holster_zone]
A light tap when your empty hand is close enough to take a holstered weapon or something you carry (grenades, objects, syringes). No tap at a holster whose weapon is in your other hand.
With "Manual holsters" on, also when a weapon in your hand stops at a free holster where it fits (letting go puts it away), once each time you bring it there. Not while you hold the weapon with both hands or aim a long weapon, so aiming with a hand near the shoulder doesn't vibrate.

# --- Crouch ---
[crouch_stick]
Push the turn stick down to crouch and up to stand up, without crouching for real.
Original game: you can only crouch for real.

[crouch_stick_threshold]
How far the turn stick must be pushed to crouch or stand up (0.3 = a light push, 0.95 = all the way).
Tip: raise it if you crouch by accident while turning.

[crouch_speed]
Walking speed while crouched, for real or with the stick (1.0 = normal walking speed).
Original game: crouching does not slow you down.

# --- Jump ---
[jump_enabled]
Push the turn stick up while standing to jump. You can climb onto tables, rocks and crates. Not under a low ceiling, swimming or skiing.
Experimental: the levels were not designed for jumping.

[jump_stick_threshold]
How far the turn stick must be pushed up to jump (0.3 = a light push, 0.95 = all the way).
With "Crouch with turn stick" on, pushing up while crouched stands you up instead.

[jump_height]
How high you jump. 100 cm is enough for tables and crates.

[jump_sound]
Sounds when you jump: an effort grunt and cloth rustle, only the cloth, or nothing.

[jump_guard]
Jumps can't take you over the level's invisible barriers, so you can't leave the playable area. Anything you can see can still be climbed.
Tip: leave it on unless you want to explore.

[land_crouch]
After a big fall you bend down on landing and come back up, with an effort grunt and a jolt in both controllers. The bigger the fall, the deeper.
If the fall hurts, the game's own pain reaction plays instead.

[land_crouch_height]
Falls from at least this height (counted from the top of a jump) are hard landings.
Only used with "Hard landings" on.

# --- Fall damage ---
[fall_damage_custom]
Falls under the safe height don't hurt and the damage grows with the height, from a scratch to a heavy hit.
Original game: the same big chunk of health for any fall from about 3.3 m, and death from 10 m.

[fall_safe_height]
Falls shorter than this don't hurt (counted from the top of a jump).
Only used with "Custom fall damage" on.

[fall_fatal_height]
Falls longer than this kill you.
Only used with "Custom fall damage" on.

[fall_damage_progressive]
Small drops hurt a little and big ones a lot.
OFF: every fall that hurts takes the game's flat amount.
Only used with "Custom fall damage" on.

[fall_damage_scale]
Damage of a fall that does not kill you, times the game's (0 = falls never hurt, 2 = twice as much).
Only used with "Custom fall damage" on.

# --- Sprint / movement ---
[sprint_mode]
Stick click: click the movement stick to sprint (original game).
Arm swing: swing your arms like a runner; faster swing, faster run.
Stick + arms: both work.
Always sprint: you run just by pushing the stick and only walk while you hold a weapon up or shoot.

[sprint_arm_sensitivity]
How much you must swing your arms to sprint. Higher: less swinging needed.
Only used with the "Arm swing" and "Stick + arms" sprint modes.

[sprint_speed_multiplier]
Top sprint speed (1.0 = the original game's).
Tip: 1.3 makes long levels less tedious without feeling unnatural.

[sprint_turn_slowdown]
Turning sharply while sprinting costs speed, which comes back over a second; a full U-turn drops you to walking pace (0 = off, 1 = full effect).

[stairs_steady_speed]
Going up or down stairs and slopes no longer speeds you up.
Original game: you move noticeably faster along stairs than on flat ground.

# --- Slide ---
[slide_enabled]
Crouch while sprinting (with the stick or for real) to slide along the floor, with a scraping sound.

[slide_strength]
How far a slide takes you (1.0 = a short slide, 2.0 = a long one).
Only used with "Slide" on.

[slide_sound]
Sound played while sliding: body on the floor, a wood or metal scrape, or nothing.
Only used with "Slide" on.

# --- Cutscenes ---
[skip_cutscenes]
Hold both triggers and both grips to skip a cutscene. The same gesture speeds through dialogue scenes where you just stand and wait.
Original game: many scenes can't be skipped.

[skip_hold_time]
How long you must hold both triggers and both grips to skip a cutscene.
Only used with "Skip cutscenes" on.

[skip_speed]
Speed of the dialogue scenes you fast-forward with the skip gesture (x normal speed).
Only used with "Skip cutscenes" on.

# --- Menu / debug ---
[menu_view]
Basic: the most important options in three categories.
Advanced: every option, including the fine-tuning ones.
Options hidden in the basic view keep working with their values.

[menu_hold_time]
How long you must hold the left Y button to open or close the in-game menu.

[debug_log]
Writes much more detail to UE4SS.log. Only useful when reporting a problem.
